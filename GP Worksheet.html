What Is Emotional Language?

Emotional language is language used to make people feel strong emotions such as happiness, fear, anger, sadness, excitement, or sympathy.

Instead of only giving facts, emotional language tries to create feelings.

Fact: “The storm damaged several houses.”
Emotional language: “The terrifying storm destroyed families’ homes and left children crying in fear.”
